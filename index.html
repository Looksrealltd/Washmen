The Laundry Chronicles
A 30-episode series about two people, one apartment, and the slow realisation that adulting in Dubai is a team sport — sponsored by the pile of clothes they've been ignoring since Tuesday.

Series arc

The Chaos
Nour and James discover that two people's laundry is not two people's laundry — it's a crisis.
Week 2
The Education
Zara introduces them to Washmen. James is sceptical. Nour is intrigued. Both are wrong about why.
Week 3
The Proof
Things come back clean. The cashmere incident happens. Trust is tested, then rebuilt.
Week 4
The Convert
James becomes the person who recommends Washmen to everyone. Nour finds this both adorable and irritating.
