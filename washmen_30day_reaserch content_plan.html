Step 2

Narrative Arc

Week 1
Awareness
Confusion, myths, friction. You're naming the pain they feel but haven't said out loud. The hook is recognition: "That's exactly my problem."
Week 2
Education
Clarity, reframing. You're pulling back the curtain. Behind-the-scenes, process shots, myth-busting. The hook is revelation: "I had no idea."
Week 3
Proof
Results, transformation. Before/afters, real customer moments, specific numbers. The hook is evidence: "Show me, don't tell me."
Week 4
Identity
Lifestyle, belonging. You're not selling laundry anymore — you're selling time, peace, status. The hook is aspiration: "That's the version of me I want to be."
